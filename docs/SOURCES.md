# Sources and attribution

## Content

**`content/endocrine_2025.yaml`** is a re-presentation of the supplied source
document `Indocrine_2025.pdf`. Every fact, figure, threshold, dose and unit on
those eleven sheets is taken from that document; terminology, numerical values
and the author's framing are preserved. Where a module reorganises the source
into a table, a diagram or a worked example, it re-presents statements that are
in the source — no external material has been added, and nothing has been
silently replaced with generic background knowledge.

Two notes on fidelity:

- The fasting blood glucose control bands (`< 108` good, `108–126` acceptable,
  `127–144` poor, `> 144` very poor) are reproduced with the units exactly as
  printed in the source, and the sheet says so on the diagram's axis label.
- The "Initial blood glucose control" figure on sheet 8 is **redrawn** in the
  notebook's visual language from the treatment figure in the source document.
  It is not an original illustration and the caption states this. No external
  image was used; the vector is generated by the `triage` diagram kind.

**`content/demo_subjects.yaml`** exists only to demonstrate that the engine is
subject-agnostic. Its content is standard textbook material on binary search and
on the Industrial Revolution in Britain.

## No external images

Every graphic in both documents is generated as vector SVG by
`engine/diagrams.py`. No third-party images were downloaded, embedded or
traced, so there is nothing to license and no watermark to consider.
